How it works

Four steps to lasting financial habits

Unlike generic finance apps, FinWise Academy is built around behavior change — not just information delivery.

1
Financial behavior quiz
A 6-question diagnostic assesses your credit habits, budgeting practices, and financial goals to generate your personal baseline profile.
2
Personalized learning path
The platform assigns priority modules and an initial action task based on your quiz results. No generic content — every path is yours.
3
Learn and act
Short 10-minute lessons end with a decision prompt and a concrete action task. Real decisions, not theory. AI coaching guides you through each step.
4
Track your habits
Your progress dashboard tracks lessons completed, action tasks done, habit streaks, and confidence over time — making the change visible.
