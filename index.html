How it works

Four steps from knowing to doing

Most financial content stops at information. FinWise Academy asks you to make a decision and take one action in every module.

1
Get your financial profile
A short quiz maps where you stand: credit, budgeting, spending habits, and goals.
2

Modules and practical activities, ordered around your priorities.

Short lessons end with a decision prompt and a concrete action task you can apply the same day.

Learning modules

Real financial skills,
built one lesson at a time

Each module connects financial concepts to real decisions you're already facing, and ends with an action task you can do today.

🏦

How the U.S. Credit System Works

Credit scores, credit history, payment behavior, and how your financial decisions affect your long-term opportunities in the United States.

💳

Credit Card Usage and Behavior

Statement cycles, utilization rates, APR vs fees, payment timing, and how to use cards without letting habits control your finances.

📊

Budgeting and Recurring Expenses

Distinguish intentional expenses from habitual ones, identify recurring costs, and plan for irregular expenses before they become financial shocks.

📋

Understanding Fees and Interest

Late fees, overdraft charges, unnecessary subscription costs, and the real price of carrying a balance month to month.

🎯

Debt Education Strategies

Practical frameworks for evaluating debt decisions, comparing repayment scenarios, and understanding the real cost of high-interest borrowing.

💰

Savings Habits and Goals

Building consistent savings habits, emergency fund strategies, and how to automate financial discipline into your daily routine.

About the founder
Marcelo Augusto Falciano
Founder, FinWise Academy

Built at the intersection of financial behavior, education, and technology

FinWise Academy was founded by Marcelo Falciano, who spent a decade in education technology and researched credit behavior before that.

🎓

The idea started at Columbia University

During his master's in Instructional Technology and Media at Teachers College, Columbia University in New York, Marcelo dug into a question that had followed him for a decade: why does financial education so rarely change what people actually do with their money? FinWise Academy grew out of that graduate work, and the program was then piloted with adult learners in the United States.

🏢

A decade scaling education technology

At Geekie, one of Brazil's leading edtech companies, Marcelo spent nearly a decade on large-scale implementation projects with schools, negotiating with institutions, and leading commercial teams.

📄

Published researcher on credit behavior

Co-author of "Effects of Credit Card Usage on Young Brazilians' Compulsive Buying" (Young Consumers, 2014). He's been studying why smart people make bad money decisions since long before founding FinWise.

🏫

51 financial-education sessions as a volunteer educator

Between 2011 and 2014, while still in college, he reached 1,858 participants across 28 institutions through the Brasilprev/Trevisan "Projeto de Vida na Ponta do Lápis" program.
